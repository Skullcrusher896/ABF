[h1] Ante Bellum: Actum [/h1]

Every time I've played Ante Bellum, I've always enjoyed the long mission trees with cultural conversion aspects, making my own head canon for the nations I play along the way. But many nations don't have the option to shape the malleable cultural identities that inhabit the world of Ante Bellum, and some corners of that world have never been explored at all.

Ante Bellum: Actum seeks to create a more lived-in and dynamic world. It gives new gameplay options to nations that have not received an update in a long time, carries the Legacy of Rome and the Heirs of Alexander to their conclusion, and opens a world lost to history: the New World settled by Carthage after Hannibal sailed west. All content is built to a single standard of quality and style, with compatibility with base AB and performance in mind.

[b]Requires:[/b] Ante Bellum

    [*] Dozens of new government mechanics, reforms, estate privileges, factions, unlockable buffs and much more!
    [*] New Great Projects, with more unlockable via mission rewards

    [*] New plagues: the Gray Fever and the Pale Death

    [*] Complete the Ostsiedlung as any German precursor, driving East and establishing the dominance of East Germanic civilization in Europe
    [*] Scandinavian nations may take to the seas once more and reshape societies and cultures forever through new naturally spreading cultures
    [*] As Sweden, complete the Northern Crusades through the creation of the Carolean Paladins and the integration of the Finns
    [*] Drive the revival of Celtic culture as Brittany/Gallia, Wales/Prydain, and Gaeldom/Ireland/Goeldelia with new naturally spreading cultures
    [*] Rise again as the Visigoths with a new mission tree
  [h2] [u]Eastern Europe[/u] [/h2]
    [*] Embrace the principles of Popular Sovereignty as Polans (Catholic Poland) and reject tyranny, with a massively expanded mission tree and new government mechanic
    [*] End the dispute over the heir to the legacy of Rus' as Lendia (Orthodox Poland), with its new fully fleshed-out formable nation: Wszechrus
    [*] Toe the fine line between Liberty and Tyranny as the Cossack Hetmanate, with a new mission tree and government mechanics
    [*] Reclaim the Eurasian Steppe as any nation of East Slavic culture, raising Cossack Hosts in former Tatar lands
    [*] Restore Slavic hegemony in Germany as Wendia
    [*] Return the Slavs to the Carpathian Basin and finish the drive East into the Baltics with Carpathian and Litvin Slavs respectively
    [*] Fight back against the encroaching Slavs as the Baltic Livonia, complete with a new mission tree and spawnable cultures
  [h2] [u]Legacy of Rome[/u] [/h2]
    [*] Restore Rome, with Roman ambitions reaching as far as East Asia, and several other nations able to attempt the Restoration of Rome
    [*] Romanize conquered lands into regional Roman cultures across the Latin, Hellenic, Persian and Sinic cultural spheres
    [*] Govern distant lands through the Diocese system, with Diocese, Grand Diocese and Foederati subjects
    [*] Face the Dragon Emperor, the Roman Civil War and the Persian War
    [*] Guide Byzantium through the Fall of the Angelids and the Crisis of the 16th Century, with new missions, events, factions and reforms
  [h2] [u]Heirs of Alexander[/u] [/h2]
    [*] Expand Nicaea into the Hellenic Empire, with new missions, events and mechanics
    [*] Hellenize your realm, rule over Diadochi subjects, and survive the Diadochi War and the Displeased Faction
    [*] New mission tree for Epirus

    [*] Lead the Punic cities through the Punic League mechanic and its reforms
    [*] Mission trees for Carthage, the Punic League nations (Dylamea, Buhutan, Kataqart) and Hylinia
    [*] New events, decisions, subject types, religion flavours and dynamic conversion
    [*] The Rise of Carthage and the Fourth Punic War
  [h2] [u]Asia[/u] [/h2]
    [*] As Tocharia, don the mantle of one of three previously dominant civilizations, becoming the true heirs of the Kushan (India), Persian, or Chinese empires, with three mission trees combined into one through unlockable missions and new government mechanics

    [*] Dominate the Indian Subcontinent as Zun, complete with a brand new mission tree, reforms, events, and more
    [*] Take the Mandate of Heaven as Japan and embrace the lasting legacy of Chinese and Mongol influence through cultural Sinicization, or challenge the status quo and promote Japanese settlement across Asia
    [*] Assert your cultural dominance as one of many Chinese claimants, completing the Sinicization of the many heavily influenced lands around you

[h1] Future Development and Credits [/h1]

Maintaining a balance between historical reference and AB's established lore, we plan to keep adding gameplay adjustments and features, as well as overhauled mission trees for nations that need more love, while staying as close to Ante Bellum's gameplay and design philosophy as we can. Upcoming focus: Eastern Europe, specifically the claimants of Rus'. Russians and Ruthenians will have separate mission trees, Perm and Tataria will receive overhauls, and much more!

Big thank you to Potion Seller (check out their great mission trees and modifications), NewYoung (ABE), the Imperium Universalis team for the Punic content, Arkerios for the Byzantine submod, and the Third Odyssey team for their content.
