[h1] Ante Bellum Flavor: Reborn - Introduction [/h1]

Every time I've played Ante Bellum, I've always enjoyed the long missions trees with cultural conversion aspects, making my own head canon for the nations I play along the way. But many nations don't have the option to shape the malleable cultural identities that inhabit the world of Ante Bellum.

Today I bring to you Ante Bellum Flavor Reborn, a full rebuild of the original mod from the ground up. Originally focused on light adjustments to Ante Bellum for the purposes of immersion and subtle gameplay enhancements, similar to Halfdan's mod. This mod seeks to create a more lived in and dynamic world, with new gameplay options, primarily for nations that have not received an update or a mini-update in a long time. The purpopse of the rebuild was to standardize the quality and style of content across the mod, as well as maximize its compatibility with other submods and base AB, as well its performance.

[b] Being rebuilt from the ground up, all old saves and compatiblity with the ultimate modpack will be BROKEN!!! [/b]

[h2] Future Development, Credits, and Heads-Up  [/h2]

Maintaining a balance between historical reference and AB's established lore, I plan on continuing to add small gameplay adjustments and features, as well as overhauled mission trees for some nations that need more love. I aim to keep my design and content aligned with Ante Bellum's gameplay and design philosophy as close as I can, while taking some creative liberty in further extending the lore. As of October 2026, the main focus on content updates of the mod will focus on expanded and overhauling Eastern Europe, specifically the claimants of Rus'. Russians and Ruthenians will have a seperate mission tree, Perm is to receive an overhaul as well as Tataria, and much more!
