# Writing for ABA

Voice target = **base AB**. Before writing for a region, read 3–5 AB entries for comparable content (Grep `$AB_PATH/localisation` for `_desc:` or `.d:` with a `head_limit`) and match them.

## AB voice

- **Mission descriptions**: first person plural ("we", "our"), 30–80 words, state the situation then the intent. Plain, confident, a touch of grandeur.
  > *"The salt mines of Rusa are a valuable resource in our trade network. By expanding it and increasing its output, we will have enough for ourselves and our trading partners abroad."*
  > *"The Kheshig Guards were originally established as bodyguards of Mongol rulers such as Genghis Khan and his wife Boerte. As a rump state of the once great Mongol Empire we should reestablish the Kheshig Guard to demonstrate our claim to Mongol greatness."*
- **Event descriptions**: 50–120 words, more dramatic; real history may be invoked briefly as context, then the stakes.
  > *"The Kingdom of England, much like the Danes and Romans before them, have been an invasive parasite on our native soil. ... No more would the petty English king be high ruler of this land..."*
- **Options**: short, in-character, often exclamatory: *"Let their chaos be our opportunity!"*, *"Our birthright has been fulfilled!"*, *"Reunited at long last!"*. System-notice events use *"Understood"*.
- **Titles**: 2–5 words, title case.
- Use AB's in-game names and spellings; real-world terms sparingly for flavour.
- No modern slang or anachronism.

## Storytelling

- A tree is a story: each column an arc, the capstone a climax; later descriptions reference earlier achievements.
- Event chains: setup → complication → payoff; carry names forward with dynamic loc.
- Branching paths should differ in tone, not only rewards.

## Proofreading (every edit)

Spelling, grammar, agreement, articles; consistent proper nouns; `§...§!` closed; `[...]` scopes valid. Existing ABA text has typos (e.g. "fierece", "unreleneting", "Finno-Urgic", "menance", "nuturing") — fix ones in lines you touch and list them; don't silently rewrite untouched text.

## Workshop page & changelogs

Steam BBCode (`[h1] [h2] [b] [i] [u] [list][*] [url=]`). Follow `README.md` (region headings, one bullet per feature, credits last) and the `newfeatures` changelog layout (region → nation → General/Reworks, Bug Fixes). Concrete: what the player can now do.
